Almendra is the single source of truth your organization has always needed but never had. Your team's knowledge lives scattered across Notion, Confluence, Google Drive, GitHub, Jira, Zendesk, and a dozen other tools — and finding the right answer means opening six tabs, skimming three wikis, and pinging the one person who might remember. Studies consistently find that knowledge workers spend 30% of their work week just searching for information. Almendra eliminates that cost by connecting all of your knowledge sources into one governed intelligence layer that your team — and your AI agents — can query for accurate, cited answers in seconds.

## The Problem Almendra Solves

Most companies operate with 8 to 12 disconnected knowledge tools. Documentation drifts out of date. Institutional knowledge lives in someone's head or buried in a Slack thread from two years ago. When your AI agents try to answer customer questions or automate workflows, they have no reliable company context to draw from — they're flying blind. Almendra gives both humans and agents a shared, governed brain they can trust.

## How Almendra Works

Almendra follows a simple three-step flow from raw documents to grounded answers.

1. **Connect your sources.** Authenticate with your existing tools — Notion, Confluence, Google Drive, GitHub, Jira, Zendesk, and more. Almendra fetches, parses, and indexes your content continuously, preserving the original permission model of each source.
2. **Ask questions in plain language.** From the dashboard or via API, ask any question in natural language. Almendra runs hybrid retrieval across your entire indexed knowledge base and synthesizes a grounded answer.
3. **Get cited, confident answers.** Every response includes a confidence score and a list of source citations — exact documents, pages, or tickets that back each claim. AI agents query the same brain through the REST API or MCP server, subject to the same permission rules that apply to human users.

    Almendra automatically surfaces questions that returned low-confidence answers, giving your team a prioritized list of documentation gaps to close.
