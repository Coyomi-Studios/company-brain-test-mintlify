## How Almendra works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your sources">
    Link your existing knowledge tools — Notion, Confluence, Google Drive, GitHub, Jira, or any URL — with your existing permissions intact. Almendra ingests and indexes everything automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask questions">
    Type a question in plain language from the dashboard or call the REST API. Almendra synthesizes an answer from across all your connected sources, with confidence scores and exact source citations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put AI agents to work">
    Any AI agent — Claude, GPT-4, Gemini, or your own — can query Almendra through the REST API or MCP server. Agents get your company's knowledge as a native tool, with the same governance rules that apply to your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Improve over time">
    Approved answers feed back into Almendra's memory. Knowledge gaps surface automatically so you always know where your documentation is thin.
  </Step>
</Steps>
